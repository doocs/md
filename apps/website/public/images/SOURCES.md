# Product images

Actual doocs/md editor screenshots captured at https://md.doocs.org/ on 2026-10-31.
Encoded as WebP for the website, preserving their actual interface and article content.

- editor-light.webp / editor-dark.webp: editor, preview and style settings.
- market-light.webp: community theme marketplace and article preview.
- ai-light.webp: actual AI conversation dialog.

These document the public Web application, not a promise that every integration has identical UI or capabilities.

## Homepage hero

`home.webp` is encoded from the user-provided `home.png` supplied on 2026-11-01.
It preserves the complete 2696 × 1440 image, including its authored background and
dark editor interface, at WebP quality 90. The same image is used in both website
appearances; it is displayed at its original aspect ratio without cropping.
