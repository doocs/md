  feedback. This clip does not show pasting into the WeChat backend.
- `themes-dark`: theme, font and spacing settings, custom CSS and article preview.
- `market-dark`: opening the theme market and previewing community themes. Public
  author names and counters are recording snapshots, not live website statistics.
- `ai-dark`: selecting article text, opening AI polish and viewing a request and its
  response. This clip does not demonstrate image generation.

The supplied edits, zooms, background and aspect ratios are preserved. Website
copies are re-encoded as H.264 MP4, CRF 20, yuv420p, constant 30 fps, with audio
removed and MP4 metadata moved to the front for progressive playback. Matching WebP
posters are extracted from the listed timestamps at quality 85. Durations are
rounded; frame-rate normalization can change the end time by one frame.

These are user-supplied MD assets.
