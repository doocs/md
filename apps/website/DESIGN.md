# Design System: md official website

## Overview

The previous design direction has been retired at the user's request. The website is being redesigned from scratch.

No visual system is currently committed. Palette, typography, layout, shapes, imagery, motion and interaction patterns will be chosen during the new design process. The existing page and previous design explorations are historical context, not requirements to preserve.

Product facts remain in `PRODUCT.md`. Record the new design system here once a direction is chosen.
